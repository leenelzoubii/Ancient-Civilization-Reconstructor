About This Project

The Ancient Civilization Reconstructor is a website where users can pick an ancient civilization and explore reconstructed environments with historical information. The project features civilizations including Ancient Egypt, Ancient Rome, the Maya Civilization, and historic sites from the Arabian Gulf region.

Users can explore 3D reconstructions, watch historical videos, and learn about the architecture, daily life, and achievements of each civilization.

The project also includes an AI-powered "broken artifact" reconstruction feature, where users can upload a photo of a broken artifact and get an AI-estimated "complete" appearance.
